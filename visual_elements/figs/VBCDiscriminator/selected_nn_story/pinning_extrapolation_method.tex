\section*{Pinning-field observables and crossing field}

We use the signed field convention $h>0$ for plaquette pinning and $h<0$
for dimer--plaquette pinning.  For each rank $r$, repeated runs at fixed
$(J_2,D,h)$ are first averaged,
\begin{equation}
 \bar C_{r,D}(J_2,h)=\frac{1}{n_D}\sum_{i=1}^{n_D}C^{(i)}_{r,D}(J_2,h),
\end{equation}
so every retained bond dimension has the same total weight, irrespective of
the number of replicas or protocols available at that $D$.

For the three sorted antiferromagnetic nearest-neighbour correlations,
\begin{equation}
 C_{\rm strong}\le C_{\rm mid}\le C_{\rm weak},
\end{equation}
we define
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_D=C_{\rm weak}-C_{\rm strong},\qquad
 f_D=\frac{C_{\rm mid}-C_{\rm strong}}
           {C_{\rm weak}-C_{\rm strong}},\qquad
 q_D=1-2f_D
 =\frac{C_{\rm weak}+C_{\rm strong}-2C_{\rm mid}}
        {C_{\rm weak}-C_{\rm strong}}.
\end{equation}
Thus $q=-1$ is dimer--plaquette, $q=+1$ is plaquette, and $\Delta\ge0$
measures the total bond splitting.

At each sampled $(J_2,h)$, the three thermodynamic correlations are obtained
independently from the unweighted two-parameter fits
\begin{equation}
 \bar C_{r,D}(J_2,h)
 =C_{r,\infty}(J_2,h)+k_r(J_2,h)e^{-a_g(J_2)D},
 \qquad r\in\{\mathrm{strong},\mathrm{mid},\mathrm{weak}\},
\end{equation}
where $a_g(J_2)$ is fixed by the original-2C3 gapped energy fit.  We test the
contiguous high-$D$ suffixes containing the two largest available bond
dimensions and use, for all three ranks, the suffix minimizing
$\max_r|C_{r,\infty}-C_{r,D_{\max}}|$; every retained $D$ has equal weight.
Only after these fits do we form
\begin{equation}
 \Delta(J_2,h)=C_{\rm weak,\infty}-C_{\rm strong,\infty},\qquad
 q(J_2,h)=
 \frac{C_{\rm weak,\infty}+C_{\rm strong,\infty}
       -2C_{\rm mid,\infty}}
      {C_{\rm weak,\infty}-C_{\rm strong,\infty}}.
\end{equation}
At $h=0$, the selected dimer--plaquette and plaquette data are fitted
separately by the same procedure, giving six extrapolated correlations.  The
corresponding ranks are then averaged,
\begin{equation}
 C_{r,\infty}(J_2,0)=\frac{C^{(d)}_{r,\infty}(J_2,0)
                              +C^{(p)}_{r,\infty}(J_2,0)}{2},
\end{equation}
and the preceding definitions give $\Delta(J_2,0)$ and $q(J_2,0)$.  No value
of either observable is imposed at $h=0$.

For the energy, replicas are first median-reduced at fixed $(J_2,D,h,s)$.
For each bond dimension and sector $s\in\{p,d\}$, the sampled fields receive
equal weight in
\begin{equation}
 E_{s,D}(J_2,h)=A_{s,D}(J_2)+B_{s,D}(J_2)h+C_{s,D}(J_2)h^2.
\end{equation}
An $h=0$ datum is omitted only when its residual from the quadratic fitted to
the nonzero fields exceeds both $3\times10^{-4}$ and six times that fit's RMS.
The two finite-$D$ branches define
\begin{equation}
 E_{p,D}(J_2,h_{c,D})=E_{d,D}(J_2,h_{c,D}),\qquad
 E_{{\rm crossing},D}=\frac{E_{p,D}(h_{c,D})+E_{d,D}(h_{c,D})}{2}.
\end{equation}
The crossing energy is extrapolated with its statistical error,
\begin{equation}
 E_{{\rm crossing},D}=E_{\rm crossing}+k_Ee^{-a_gD},\qquad
 \chi^2=\sum_D\frac{[E_{{\rm crossing},D}-E_{\rm crossing}
 -k_Ee^{-a_gD}]^2}{\sigma_{E,D}^2},
\end{equation}
where $a_g(J_2)$ is fixed by the original-2C3 energy fit.  Its covariance,
reduced-$\chi^2$ scatter, and the archived uncertainty of $a_g$ give
$\sigma_{E_{\rm crossing}}$.

The crossing fields are combined without a $D$-fit.  With
\begin{equation}
 w_D=\frac{1}{\sigma_{h,D}\,|E_{{\rm crossing},D}-E_{\rm crossing}|},
 \qquad p_D=\frac{w_D}{\sum_{D'}w_{D'}},\qquad
 h_c=\sum_Dp_Dh_{c,D},
\end{equation}
the asymmetric $1\sigma$ interval is the $15.87\%$--$84.13\%$ interval of
$P(h)=\sum_Dp_D\,\mathcal N(h_{c,D},\sigma_{h,D})$.
